Accessibility
------------------------------

To help you create accessible experiments and surveys, we have created specific documentation and demo experiments that consider the accessibility of different populations or clinical groups.

Accessible experiments
==============================
You can find recommendations, demo experiments, and further information on how to create experiments that are accessible to all here: :ref:`<_accessexperiments>`. 

Accessible surveys
==============================
You can find recommendations and further information on how to create surveys that are accessible to all here: :ref:`<_accesssurveys>`.

In addition, we are working on creating documentation on how PsychoPy Studio can be accessible to all researchers. Come back soon; you will find that information here!
